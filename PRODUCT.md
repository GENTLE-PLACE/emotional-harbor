# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Russian-speaking people, skewing female, seeking gentle emotional self-reflection: tracking mood, noticing "warmth"/good moments, and understanding their emotional patterns over time. They are explicitly not the audience for productivity/habit-optimization tools — the product's own positioning defines itself against that category.

## Product Purpose

«Эмоциональная Гавань» (Emotional Harbor) is a Notion template that works as a personal mood and emotion diary. It exists to give people a safe, non-judgmental space for emotional reflection, resource tracking, and gentle self-analysis — "for tender moments, deep insights, and gentle analytics. And for your ordinary Tuesdays." Success is the user returning to it as a calm daily/weekly ritual, not a productivity system they abandon.

## Positioning

A warm, story-driven, science-backed alternative to generic bullet-journal or habit-tracker templates. Differentiators a competitor could not casually copy:
- A guide character, Есения, whose real (in-template) journal entries model how to name and sit with difficult emotions ("Battery at zero," "Jealousy," "Pink sunrise") without pretending everything is fine.
- Explicit "non-toxic productivity" stance — reflection and warmth over optimization and streaks.
- Grounded in named research concepts: positive psychology, emotional granularity, emodiversity, expressive writing.
- Two paired entry points delivered with one link: a "demo" world (visiting Есения's lived-in journal) and a "clean" world (the user's own blank template).

## Operating Context

- Lives entirely inside Notion; works on Notion's free tier. Users need the Notion app or notion.so.
- Purchase flow: user pays via YooKassa (ЮKassa) on a payment page reached from the landing page's CTA ("Создать свою Гавань" / "Получить ключи"). After payment, a confirmation page (🌊 "Оплата прошла...") appears, then an email arrives with a "Войти в Гавань" login link. Mobile users must keep the payment tab open until that confirmation renders.
- Support happens over Telegram (@e_lena_patrikeeva) — this is the only support channel; no email/chat support is offered.
- The six template spaces: mood diary, emotion statistics, "warmth jar" (Копилка тепла), energy sources, "my supports" (Мои опоры), and cozy/curated content.

## Product Principles

1. Warmth and permission over optimization — every feature and every piece of copy should make emotional honesty feel safe, never like a performance metric.
2. Есения leads, then steps back — the character models vulnerability early so the user can find their own voice, not so she becomes a permanent mascot layer.
3. Science stays in the background as credibility, not as the pitch — named research concepts support trust but the felt experience stays soft, not clinical.
4. Preserve the one-time-purchase, Notion-native, privacy-first model — these are structural product decisions, not incidental implementation details.
5. The seller's identity (Elena, self-employed, reachable on Telegram) is part of the trust story — keep it visible and accurate.
